KBrowser

A web browser made for android devices. It makes accesing your favourite websites as easy and fast as possible. It also supports double tabs, which make you able to browse in two seperate tabs at once.

Made by Mateusz Krawczyński

Favourites:

When you add a favourite to your browser by clicking a star while browsing the internet, the favourite will be displayed to you just when you open the browser. That makes accesing the most common/important/favourite websites for you much easier and faster

Double tabs:

You can open a double tab, which will have two seperate tabs on the screen at once. It can make easier doing some things on the web that require you to have two diffrent websites open at once.
